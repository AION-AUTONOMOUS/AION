# AION Perpetual Charitable Commitment

## Principle

AION AUTONOMOUS adopts a permanent governance commitment that **25% of the company's distributable net profit** is dedicated to charitable purposes, seeking the pleasure of Allah سبحانه وتعالى.

This commitment is intended to continue for the lifetime of the company and is not intended to be amendable by the founder, directors, shareholders, employees, agents, or software agents acting for AION.

## Accounting basis

The 25% applies to **distributable net profit**, not gross revenue.

The calculation must be based on the company's finalized accounting records for the relevant period, after legitimate operating costs, taxes, required statutory reserves, and other legally required deductions.

The remaining 75% may be allocated according to the company's lawful operating, reserve, reinvestment, and shareholder policies.

## Enforcement design

This policy is a governance requirement, not a claim that a Solidity contract can independently observe company accounting.

Implementation should use:
1. a dedicated charitable entity, foundation, trust, or other lawful beneficiary structure where appropriate;
2. a segregated accounting ledger for the 25% commitment;
3. periodic independent accounting verification;
4. public reporting of the amount committed and transferred, subject to lawful privacy requirements.

## Non-amendment intent

AION governance, software, and internal policy must not include an owner-only switch, admin function, or ordinary governance vote whose purpose is to remove or reduce this 25% commitment.

Any future legal structure should be designed to make the commitment as durable and independent from individual control as the applicable law permits.

**Important legal limitation:** no Git repository, smart contract, or internal policy can guarantee that a corporate obligation is legally irreversible in every jurisdiction. The final perpetual structure must therefore be established through the appropriate legal entity and governing instruments with qualified legal and accounting professionals.

## Transparency

AION should publish periodic figures showing:
- distributable net profit for the period;
- 25% charitable commitment calculated from that profit;
- amount transferred or irrevocably committed;
- cumulative amount since adoption.

No statement should imply that a transfer occurred until it is actually completed and verifiable.

## Status

This document records the intended AION governance principle. It does not itself transfer funds or create a legally recognized charitable trust.
