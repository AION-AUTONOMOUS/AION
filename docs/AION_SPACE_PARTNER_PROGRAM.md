# AION Space — Satellite Partner Program

## Purpose

AION Space is designed to sell **satellite-derived intelligence products**, not raw imagery alone. AION combines customer acquisition, mission orchestration, AI analysis, verification, reporting, alerts, and APIs; the satellite partner supplies authorized Earth-observation data and/or tasking.

## Target partner model

### Primary target: Planet
Target relationship: **Value Added Reseller / Platform-oriented partnership** through Planet Orbit.

Planet's published Partner Program recognizes Platform Partners, Reseller Partners, and Value Added Resellers. A Value Added Reseller may distribute Planet Products and create/distribute Derivative Products for named end users under the applicable agreements and Order Schedules.

### Secondary target: ICEYE
Target relationship: **Data Reseller / Solutions Partner**.

ICEYE publicly states that it works with data resellers authorized to distribute ICEYE SAR imagery to customers or use it to create customer solutions. ICEYE also has solutions-reseller examples where partner geospatial services are combined with ICEYE insights.

## AION commercial target

This is a **negotiation target, not an existing supplier commitment**:

- AION economic share: **>= 75%**
- Satellite/data provider economic share: **<= 25%**
- Prefer customer-funded missions rather than speculative imagery inventory.
- Prefer commercial terms that align supplier payment with successful customer collection, subject to the partner contract.

The 75/25 target must never be represented to customers or suppliers as an already-agreed rate until a signed agreement establishes it.

## Commercial guardrails

1. No satellite purchase or launch is implied.
2. No paid imagery/tasking is ordered without an authorized commercial agreement and customer-funded business case.
3. No claim of a 75/25 split is made until signed.
4. Data licenses, derivative-product rights, territory, named end users, AI/ML use, redistribution, retention, and SLA must be verified in the supplier agreement.
5. AION must not represent itself as owning or operating a partner's satellite constellation.
6. Customer-facing products should emphasize AION's analysis and decision-support value rather than implying ownership of source satellite assets.
