# AION AI Asset Registry

## Purpose
The AION Global Asset Vault now has a deterministic registry model for **10,000,000 registered AI roles** across ten asset classes.

This is a registry of capabilities and identities. It is **not** a claim that 10,000,000 model processes are running concurrently.

## Allocation
Each asset class has exactly 1,000,000 registered roles:

1. Equities & Funds
2. Bonds & Fixed Income
3. Real Estate
4. Precious Metals & Commodities
5. Energy & Infrastructure
6. Usage Rights & Licenses
7. Intellectual Property
8. Data & Data Rights
9. Digital Assets & Tokenized RWA
10. Environmental & Energy Certificates

## Root identifiers
- Vault root: `000000000001`
- Prime Intelligence root: `999999999999`

These are identifiers only. They are not balances, assets, ownership certificates, or money.

## Role families
Each registered role can be assigned a capability such as Scout, Analyst, Verifier, Risk, Valuation, Evidence, Guardian, Audit, Economic, or Learning.

Every role has:
- no ownership authority
- no financial authority
- evidence requirement
- verification requirement

## Real-asset rule
An AI role may discover, analyze, reconcile, and monitor an asset record, but it cannot create legal ownership merely by writing to AION software.

The trusted chain remains:

**Real Asset → External Registry/Custodian → Evidence → AI Verification → Vault Record → Economic Intelligence**

Unverified or mismatched evidence must not be promoted to verified ownership or real revenue.
