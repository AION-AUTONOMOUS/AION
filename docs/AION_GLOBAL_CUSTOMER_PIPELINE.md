## Commercial offer

AION proposes a non-exclusive success-fee partnership:

- 5% of the qualifying contract value for business introduced or materially facilitated by AION.
- Renewal/expansion commission only when expressly included in the signed agreement.
- No upfront provider fee for the initial proposition.
- Provider retains technical control and final customer acceptance.
- AION supplies demand generation, qualification, matching, commercial coordination and auditable referral evidence.

## Customer acquisition engine

AION should generate qualified RFQs rather than mass spam:

- Industry-specific landing pages.
- Requirement intake: location, service type, coverage, bandwidth/resolution, latency, term, SLA, budget and deployment date.
- Automated qualification.
- Multi-provider matching.
- Human/legal approval only where required by regulation, export control, government procurement or contract authority.
- CRM-style pipeline: lead → qualified → RFQ → provider match → quote → negotiation → contract → commission → renewal.

## Evidence

Commercial demand is broad across agriculture, insurance, energy, maritime, infrastructure and government. Saudi CST reports substantial EO demand driven by government/military, oil & gas and environmental applications. Saudi Arabia also has a licensed national EO marketplace, so AION should differentiate as a cross-provider commercial orchestration/referral layer rather than simply another imagery catalogue.
