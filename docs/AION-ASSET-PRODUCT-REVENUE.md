# AION Asset Intelligence Product & Revenue Boundary

The product engine transforms verified Asset Vault intelligence into a measurable service product.

Flow:

Verified Asset → Economic Intelligence → Product → Customer Purchase → Revenue Recognition

A proposed service price is not revenue.

Revenue is recognized only after a real customer purchase is confirmed by an authorized payment/commerce system.

The engine never:
- invents asset values;
- infers legal ownership from registry presence;
- reports a customer;
- reports revenue without a real purchase.

This boundary allows AION to automate product generation while keeping financial truth auditable.
