# AION Frontier Architecture

## Mission
AION is being built as an autonomous technology platform with four compounding layers:

1. **AION Intelligence** — model orchestration, reasoning, memory, tools and evaluations.
2. **AION Autonomous Company** — departments, agents, planning, execution, observability and governance.
3. **AION Research** — reproducible experiments and frontier-intelligence research.
4. **AION Economy & Network** — AION-CREDIT internal economy first, followed by a separately governed token/network architecture.

## Operating principle
AION should execute ordinary engineering, research, operations and product work autonomously. Actions involving external money, custody, sensitive assets, legal commitments, political targeting or mainnet deployment remain fail-closed and require the owner approval gate.

## Intelligence loop
Goal -> decompose -> retrieve evidence -> reason -> delegate -> execute through real adapters -> verify independently -> record audit -> learn from outcome.

## Intelligence planes
- Planner: decomposes goals into verifiable tasks.
- Router: selects department, agent role and tools.
- Memory: durable facts, task state, artifacts and lessons.
- Executor: bounded real-world adapters.
- Verifier: tests, evidence checks and independent validation.
- Evaluator: benchmarks model/tool/agent performance over time.
- Research Lab: experiments with reproducible datasets, prompts, policies and model configurations.

## Frontier research
AION may research AGI/ASI pathways, but must not claim AGI or superintelligence without independent evidence. The research system should measure capabilities instead of relying on labels.

## Economy/network progression
Phase 1: internal AION-CREDIT ledger and accounting.
Phase 2: economic simulation, supply/fee/governance/security models.
Phase 3: testnet-compatible protocol and contract suite.
Phase 4: independent security review and operational readiness.
Phase 5: mainnet only after explicit owner authorization and required compliance/security checks.

## Non-negotiable invariants
- No fake execution.
- No fake balances.
- No secret values in logs.
- No autonomous external money movement.
- No unreviewed mainnet deployment.
- No political targeting.
- Research claims must be evidence-backed.
