# AION VERITY — Intellectual Property Protection and Ownership Policy

**Project:** AION AUTONOMOUS GLOBAL NETWORK  
**Product:** AION VERITY — Verifiable Information Provenance  
**Status:** Internal project policy and ownership intent; not a patent grant, legal assignment, trademark registration, or proof of incorporation.

## 1. Ownership intent

AION intends to develop and control AION VERITY as a proprietary product line within AION AUTONOMOUS GLOBAL NETWORK. Project materials, original source code, specifications, designs, documentation, and future inventions should be inventoried and protected for the benefit of the properly identified AION legal owner.

This statement records project intent only. It does not by itself transfer rights from any person, establish that AION is an incorporated legal entity, or override third-party rights. Before asserting legal ownership or filing an application, identify the legal applicant/owner and document any necessary assignments from actual human inventors, contributors, contractors, or other rights holders.

## 2. Access and disclosure rules

- Treat non-public implementation details, security architecture, keys, credentials, and unreleased technical designs as confidential.
- Do not place secrets, credentials, private keys, personal data, or confidential patent-enabling details in public issues or public repositories.
- Before sharing confidential implementation details with outside parties, use an appropriate written confidentiality agreement reviewed for the relevant jurisdiction.
- Limit repository and service access to authorized accounts; enable multi-factor authentication, least privilege, and periodic access review.
- Keep a dated inventory of contributors, commits, dependencies, licenses, invention notes, test results, and disclosure history.
- Do not claim that a public GitHub repository is private or that GitHub access controls create patent rights.

## 3. Patent protection workflow

Before public disclosure of further technical details:
1. Identify the concrete technical mechanism proposed for protection; an abstract goal such as “prove truth” is not itself a patent specification.
2. Search prior art and relevant patent literature.
3. Record the actual human inventor(s) and confirm the appropriate applicant and chain of title.
4. Have a qualified patent professional review novelty, inventive step, patent eligibility, disclosure sufficiency, jurisdictions, and filing strategy.
5. Prepare the abstract, full description, claims, and drawings where required.
6. Obtain the owner's explicit approval for the applicant, scope, jurisdictions, and costs before filing.
7. Preserve the official receipt, application number, filing date, and subsequent office correspondence.

A patent is not granted merely by drafting this policy or committing code. Patent rights and enforcement depend on the relevant office, law, claim scope, and status of the application.

## 4. Other protection

- **Copyright:** retain authorship and commit history for original code and documents; audit repository licenses and third-party components.
- **Trademark:** assess and, if authorized, register AION VERITY and related marks in selected jurisdictions.
- **Trade secrets:** preserve secrecy through access controls, confidentiality obligations, and data minimization.
- **Contracts:** use written IP assignment and confidentiality agreements with contributors where lawful and appropriate.

## 5. Third-party rights and open-source review

Do not copy third-party code, standards text, media, or patented implementations without checking the applicable license and rights. Maintain a dependency and license inventory. Interoperability with existing standards may be necessary and does not imply ownership of those standards.

## 6. Public-disclosure caution

The current proposal and some project discussion have already been published in the public AION GitHub repository. A patent professional should assess exactly what was disclosed, when, and whether it affects novelty in each target jurisdiction. Do not assume a grace period applies. Do not publish further enabling details until this review is completed.

## 7. Prohibited claims until supported by official evidence

Do not state that AION VERITY is patented, patent-pending, legally exclusive worldwide, or immune from copying unless the exact statement is supported by official filing/grant records and legal review. A filed application is not a granted patent. A trademark application is not a registered mark. A repository commit is not a legal assignment.

## 8. Approval and costs

No paid filing, attorney engagement, trademark application, paid search, or external service is authorized by this policy. Obtain explicit approval before any expense or binding legal submission.
