# AION Patent / IP Invention Disclosure — Candidate

## Working title

AION Autonomous Multi-Orbit Capacity Exchange and Verifiable Space-Service Orchestration System

## Problem

Commercial satellite capacity and Earth-observation services are fragmented across operators, constellations, APIs, distributors and service contracts. Customer requirements are expressed in business terms while provider inventories expose different technical and commercial parameters.

## Candidate technical invention

A computer-implemented orchestration system that builds a normalized capability graph for heterogeneous space assets and services; transforms a customer requirement into machine-verifiable constraints; continuously evaluates multi-provider and multi-orbit candidates; produces an explainable ranked allocation/quotation plan; and creates a tamper-evident transaction record linking the requirement, eligible offers, constraints, provider response, acceptance and commercial entitlement.

Potential technical components:

1. Capability normalization schema for heterogeneous satellite/service inventories.
2. Constraint engine combining spatial, temporal, orbital, bandwidth, latency, SLA, price and authorization constraints.
3. Multi-agent matching pipeline with bounded specialist agents.
4. Explainable match evidence rather than opaque ranking.
5. Cryptographically chained evidence for offer/quote/acceptance/commission events.
6. Provider-specific adapters that never expose credentials or unauthorized tasking capability.
7. Separation of customer identity from sensitive task/coverage requirements where appropriate.
8. Continuous post-contract monitoring of delivery/SLA/availability and commission state.

## Prior-art warning

A preliminary search already found existing patents covering satellite tasking, secure/private satellite tasking, and satellite capacity allocation. Therefore AION must not file a broad claim such as "AI marketplace for satellites" without professional claim drafting and a full prior-art/FTO analysis.

Examples found:
- US12244575B2, Method and system for satellite tasking.
- US12652160, Anonymous/authenticated/private satellite tasking system.
- CN111491382B, Satellite capacity allocation method, device and electronic equipment.

The differentiation must be tied to concrete technical mechanisms actually implemented by AION.

## Protection plan

1. Freeze and date this invention disclosure internally.
2. Maintain confidential source-code/design records and inventor contribution records.
3. Do not publicly disclose a potentially patentable implementation before counsel confirms the filing strategy.
4. Commission a professional patentability/prior-art search.
5. Prepare claims, drawings and implementation description with a qualified patent professional.
6. Consider an initial national filing followed by a PCT route where commercially appropriate.
7. Protect complementary non-patentable assets as trade secrets, copyright, trademarks and contractual know-how.
