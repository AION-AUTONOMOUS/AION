# AION Asset Economic Intelligence

This layer converts integrity-verified Asset Vault records into measurable economic intelligence.

Flow:

Asset Vault → Registry Integrity → Economic Metrics → Portfolio Intelligence → Product/Economic Systems

Measured fields include known valuation, known cash flow and annualized cash-flow yield when both inputs exist.

Rules:
- Missing valuation is not invented.
- Missing cash flow is not invented.
- Registry records do not create legal ownership.
- Asset value is not revenue.
- No customer purchase is recognized as revenue by this module.
- Integrity failures are rejected.
- Every output receives a deterministic fingerprint for auditability.

The module is an intelligence layer, not a custodian, broker, bank, accountant, or legal title system.
