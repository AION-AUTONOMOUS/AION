# AION Legal Formation — zero-upfront operating plan

## Decision
Use Estonia as the target legal domicile because its official e-Residency programme supports remote company administration and online OÜ registration.

## Important cost boundary
Government registration is not free: the current official OÜ registration fee is €265, and e-Residency application is €150. The e-Residency application must be paid when submitted; Estonia does not offer payment-later for that fee.

## Zero-upfront strategy
AION can operate as a pre-incorporation software project while revenue is being generated, provided contracts, invoicing, tax, consumer, employment and regulated activities are handled under the founder's applicable legal status until incorporation. No government filing is claimed before it is actually submitted and accepted.

## Deferred capital
Estonia permits the minimum OÜ share-capital contribution to be €0.01 per shareholder, subject to the official registration rules. This does not eliminate the state registration fee.

## What AION can prepare autonomously
- company name and business description research
- EMTAK activity research
- draft articles and founder resolutions
- ownership/cap table draft
- privacy, terms, security and compliance documentation
- incorporation checklist
- provider/contact-person comparison
- post-incorporation banking and accounting checklist

## What requires the owner
- identity verification
- e-Residency application submission and payment
- digital signatures
- government registration payment
- KYC/banking approval
- regulated licences and statutory declarations

AION must never claim legal incorporation until an official registry returns a company number/certificate.
