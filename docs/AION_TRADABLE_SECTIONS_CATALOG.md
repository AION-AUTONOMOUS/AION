# AION Tradable Sections & Commercial Catalog
**Status:** Proposed catalog and readiness gates. This document does not create securities, list assets on an exchange, activate payments, or certify any product as production-ready.

## Objective
Organize AION's business lines into a clear catalog of products and services that can be offered commercially. Every listing must have a real deliverable, owner, price, evidence, order path, confirmed payment, and delivery record before it is marked available.

## Commercial status labels
- **Concept:** idea only; not for sale.
- **Prototype:** demonstrable early version; no production claims.
- **Validation:** being tested; not generally available.
- **Ready for sale:** delivery, support, security, legal review, and payment flow have passed documented acceptance checks.
- **Paused:** blocked by technical, legal, data, or operational requirements.

Do not infer status from a name, page, branch, or specification.

## Catalog E — AION Coin and digital assets
AION Coin is a separate digital-asset workstream, not a share in the company and not automatically a tradable security. No claim of mainnet launch, exchange listing, liquidity, or market price is permitted without verifiable on-chain and exchange evidence.

Before any mainnet deployment or liquidity action:
- verify the reviewed contract and distribution;
- reconcile six required vault roles against the seven addresses supplied;
- review current PRs, tests, ownership, and deployment permissions;
- use secure wallet practices and never expose private keys or recovery phrases;
- obtain legal review for the target jurisdictions;

Do not merge tokenomics changes without explicit approval. Do not imply AION Coin represents ownership, equity, dividends, or a claim on company revenue unless a compliant legal structure explicitly establishes that.

## What “tradable” means in this catalog
For software and digital services, it means commercially orderable and deliverable, not exchange-traded.
For a financial instrument, token, share, or investment product, it means legally eligible for the relevant offering and venue, with required approvals and disclosures. This document does not issue, tokenize, list, or authorize any financial instrument.

## Mandatory listing record
Every item must record:
- Product/service name and business owner.
- Current status and last verification date.
- Public product URL and sample/demo.
- Price, currency, taxes, and refund terms.
- Data/IP rights and source provenance.
- Order ID and payment-provider confirmation reference.
- Delivery evidence and customer support path.
- Security/privacy review and legal restrictions.
- Known blockers and rollback/withdrawal process.

## Payment integrity
- A browser redirect is not proof of payment.
- Verify provider-side order/capture status and signed webhook events.
- Use idempotency to prevent duplicate orders/revenue.
- Do not deliver paid products before confirmed payment.
- Do not report revenue until the provider confirms it and the order is reconciled.
- Keep sandbox and live credentials separate.
- Never place secrets in source control or logs.

## Definition of success
A section is commercially operational only when its deliverable, terms, order path, payment confirmation, delivery evidence, support, and required approvals are verified. A catalog document alone does not make a product operational or tradable.
