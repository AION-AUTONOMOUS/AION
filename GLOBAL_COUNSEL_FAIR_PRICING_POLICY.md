# AION GLOBAL COUNSEL — Fair & Transparent Pricing Policy
Version: 1.0
Date: 2026-10-09
Status: Proposed public pricing; not yet connected to checkout or payment processing.

## Principles
- Access to basic legal information must not depend on ability to pay.
- Prices describe a defined information/drafting scope, not the value of a person's rights.
- No guaranteed case outcomes, court acceptance, or substitute for a licensed local lawyer.
- The same source-verification standard applies to low-cost and premium tiers.
- Show total price, deliverables, jurisdiction, exclusions, turnaround estimate, and refund/cancellation terms before payment.
- Do not charge or claim payment until the payment provider confirms a transaction and the order ledger records verifiable evidence.
- Do not accept confidential case files until authentication, private storage, encryption/key management, malware scanning, access isolation, deletion controls, and independent security tests pass.
- Pricing is not a statement that legal practice is licensed in every jurisdiction. Human legal representation or jurisdiction-specific advice may only be offered through appropriately authorized professionals.

## Hardship access
- A limited number of free cases may be offered when operational capacity allows; availability must be stated honestly.
- Users may request a hardship waiver; do not demand unnecessary sensitive proof.
- Never imply that a waiver guarantees a lawyer or a legal outcome.

## Large companies, sovereign/public-sector matters, and cross-border disputes
- Initial scoping and conflict/jurisdiction triage: $250–$750, credited toward a later engagement where agreed.
- Research memorandum for a defined multi-jurisdiction question: from $1,500 after scope, jurisdictions, sources, and review responsibilities are agreed.
- Complex international dispute, state-to-state matter, treaty analysis, major arbitration, or high-value transaction: custom written quote, typically starting at $3,000; qualified local/cross-border counsel and licensed providers are separate unless expressly included.
- No claim of government mandate, legal representation, acquisition authority, or guaranteed result without documented authorization.

## Payment and scope safeguards
1. Display the exact deliverable and fixed price before checkout.
2. State taxes, currency, page limits, revisions, turnaround assumptions, exclusions, and refund terms clearly.
3. Obtain explicit consent before any recurring subscription; provide a clear cancellation path.
4. Never initiate paid third-party services or spend without the customer's consent and the owner's authorization where required.
5. A payment is recognized as revenue only after provider-confirmed payment and verifiable transaction evidence.
6. No payment button may be represented as operational until an end-to-end test confirms checkout, provider confirmation/webhook, order state, receipt, and refund/cancellation behavior.

## Required disclaimer
AION GLOBAL COUNSEL provides legal information, research organization, and drafting assistance within the scope disclosed for each offer. It is not a substitute for a licensed lawyer and does not promise a result. Laws, deadlines, procedure, and authorization requirements vary by jurisdiction. Urgent deadlines, criminal matters, safety risks, and court filings should be reviewed promptly by a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.
